01 / ONE TAP, START TO FINISH

Scroll to follow one drink.

  1. 01
    Ready

    The float sensor says there is liquid in the tank. Green light on, and the display invites the next guest.

  2. 02
    Tap

    The bracelet's tag touches the reader. The Arduino reads its ID and looks up the balance.

  3. 03
    Pay

    15 credits is enough for a 6-credit shot. The new balance, 9, goes straight to the display.

  4. 04
    Pour

    A transistor switches the 6 V pump on for five seconds while the green light blinks.

  5. 05
    Enjoy

    The pump stops, the last drops fall, and the display says the only thing left to say.

    A PROTOTYPE, NOT A PRODUCT

    What works.
    What I would change.

    The prototype proves the tap-to-pour idea end to end. A dispenser for a real festival needs more than that.

    01 / BALANCES

    Store them properly.

    Two balances live in the code and reset when the power does. Next: keep them on the tag or on a server.

    02 / WAITING

    React at once.

    The loop sleeps five seconds between checks, so a tap can wait. Next: poll the reader without blocking.

    03 / THE POUR

    Measure, do not time.

    Five seconds of pumping is not a fixed volume as the tank empties. Next: a flow sensor.

    04 / THE REAL WORLD

    Age and hygiene.

    Serving alcohol needs age checks, food-safe parts and a sturdier case than cardboard.
