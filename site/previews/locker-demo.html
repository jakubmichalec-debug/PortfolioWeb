      PHYSICALLY INSIDE 02

      What’s real, what’s simulated

      This walkthrough adds teaching safeguards: move the item before closing, no time limit while you learn, non-empty descriptions and duplicate-tag checks. Waits after a rejected card are shortened by default (untick Quick feedback for the real delays). The real Arduino sketch has no door or item sensor: it runs an eight-second servo window and removes the first matching record afterward. Those physical-step safeguards would need extra hardware and firmware in a real locker. Card IDs, owner checks and staff registration are based on the saved sketch; geometry, sample descriptions and exposed latch placement are illustrative. The guided view adds helpful LCD prompts and starts closed at 90°, while the original setup writes 0°. This is not a secure production system.
